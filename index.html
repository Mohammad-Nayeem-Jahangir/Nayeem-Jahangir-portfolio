Research & Publications
2025 — Wiley
Machine Learning Techniques for Demand Forecasting in the Electricity Sector
Authors: Nahid, F. A.; Chowdhury, H. M.; Jahangir, M. N.  ·  Forecasting in Smart Grids, pp. 131–172
Reviews ML approaches for electricity demand forecasting critical to smart grid operation. Evaluates time-series models, regression, ensemble learning, deep learning, and hybrid algorithms including a case study using ConvLSTM on Thailand's metropolitan electricity data. ML techniques show higher accuracy and adaptability over traditional methods.
DOI: 10.1002/9781394249466.ch6
2025 — Wiley
Evaluation and Performance Metrics for Forecasting Renewable Power Generation, Demand, and Electricity Price
Authors: Nahid, F. A.; Jahangir, M. N.; Chowdhury, H. M.; Akter, K.  ·  Forecasting in Smart Grids, pp. 173–218
Addresses the lack of standardized evaluation metrics for renewable energy forecasting. Provides comprehensive review of statistical metrics (MAE, RMSE) and domain-specific measures, examining their applicability, reliability, and interpretability to support better decision-making in renewable energy systems.
DOI: 10.1002/9781394249466.ch7
2023 — University of Malaya
Partial Shading Effect on Power Generation of BIPV System in Bangladesh
Authors: Jahangir, M. N.; Mia, Md. S.; Chowdhury, S.  ·  Int. Journal of Renewable Energy Resources, 13(2), pp. 49–57
Experimental and simulation study investigating partial shading impacts on building-integrated PV systems in Bangladesh. Using paper coverage (~50% shading) and MATLAB simulations, found that avoiding partial shading can increase power output by up to 41%. Key factors identified include irradiance, temperature, and ideality factor.
DOI: 10.22452/ijrer.vol13no2.2
2019 — MANTECH Publications
Solar Radiation Forecasting Using Hybrid Convolutional Long Short-Term Memory Neural Network
Authors: Nahid, F. A.; Chowdhury, H. M.; Jahangir, M. N.  ·  Journal of Research in Physics and Applied Sciences, 2(2), pp. 1–13
Developed a hybrid CLSTM neural network for 30-minute-ahead solar radiation forecasting. The 1D CNN component extracts spatial/temporal features while LSTM handles long-term dependencies. Trained on 2 years of irradiance, temperature, and humidity data. The CLSTM outperforms standalone RNN and CNN models on MAE, RMSE, and MAPE metrics.
DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.3768721
